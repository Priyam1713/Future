# Tarot — symbolic reading

**Symbolic interpretation;no certain prediction or diagnosis.**

Profile: waite-1922-text-v1; spread: waite_celtic

Question/context: What symbolic themes could help me reflect on a creative project?

The central atmosphere (valour;discussion;competition) meets the crossing motif (attraction;love;trials overcome). Compare the aim (announcements;indecision) with the established basis (injustice;double dealing),then read the passing and approaching influences in order. The right-hand staff moves from stance through environment and hopes/fears to a symbolic culmination (prudence;discernment;accomplishment). This is a compositional reading,not a forecast or a source-documented card-pair rule.
