### Source-backed symbolic reading

**Hand shape**
- Cheiro associates this formation with: Instinct,artistic responsiveness and impulsive enthusiasm. [direct; palm.cheiro.shape.conic v1; Part I,chVI,p37]
  Scope: General type only; finger subdivisions and strong-head-line modification of mixed type are not synthesized.

**Life line**
- Cheiro associates this formation with: A close Head/Life junction is associated with caution and sensitivity in pursuing personal plans. [direct; palm.cheiro.life.gap.joined v1; Part II,chV,p80; chVII,pp88,90; joined:Plate XVI d-d]

**Head line**
- Cheiro associates this formation with: A slightly sloping Head line is associated with imaginative work; the source makes its expression dependent on hand type. [direct; palm.cheiro.head.sloping v1; Part II,chVII,pp88-89; upward branches:Part II,chIII,p76]
  Scope: General reading only; sloping lines have different significance with square versus conic/psychic hand types (p87).

**Heart line**
- Cheiro associates this formation with: A Heart line rising from central Jupiter is associated with steady,idealistic affection. [direct; palm.cheiro.heart.origin.jupiter v1; Part II,chX,p98]
  Scope: Source uses rise/origin on thumb side; do not confuse it with the little-finger end. Gender/class stereotypes omitted.
- Cheiro associates this formation with: A small fork on Jupiter is associated with sincerity and enthusiasm in affection. [direct; palm.cheiro.heart.small_fork v1; Part II,chX,p100; Plate XVI j-j (scan132)]

## Limits
- Historic symbolic interpretation; not evidence of personality, health, longevity or future outcomes.
- Observations are supplied by a person; no photo analysis or independent verification of their accuracy.
- Only Cheiro Ninth Edition procedures selected here; no Benham, Saint-Germain or Indian rules substituted.
- Missing/uncertain values remain unevaluated; no inference from an absent line to ability or character.
- Life-line length,depth and breaks are recorded without health,death or accident interpretations.
- Minor markings,elementary type,dermatoglyphics,timing and broad whole-person judgments are unsupported.
- Shape subtypes,mount leaning/excess and mixed-hand strong-Head modifications remain unsupported.
