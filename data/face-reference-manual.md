## Historical terminology
physiognomy: Hunter's translation uses physiognomy for the proposed science and physionomy for outward appearance; this records terminology,not scientific endorsement. (Volume I,Fragment III,p20,footnote; canvas60).
pathognomy: Lavater contrasted fixed/resting form with features in motion,calling the latter pathognomy. No emotion,character or internal state is inferred by this engine. (Volume I,Fragment IV,p23; canvas63).
