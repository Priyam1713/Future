Original research notes, 2026-11-02. Source: U.S. Naval Observatory, Converting Between Julian Dates and Gregorian Calendar Dates, Formula for Conversion and Introduction.

The calendar formula accepts Gregorian dates from 1801 through 2099 and UT1 hours. Integer truncation is required. The pre-March-1900 correction must remain present. Do not silently substitute UTC for UT1.

Published examples: 1877-08-11 at 7.5 UT1 hours maps to JD 2406842.8125. 1978-01-01 at 0 UT1 hours maps to JD 2443509.5. 1978-07-21 at 15 UT1 hours maps to JD 2443711.125.

These facts support calendar calculations. They supply no astrological interpretations or evidence of predictive accuracy.
