# Palmistry Block 14

Capture and Examine use a browser-local photo or a no-photo manual fallback. The guide highlights anatomical regions on an illustration, while the visitor compares it with their own palm. Capturing stops the camera tracks. Removal or leaving the room discards the image, and confirmation sends only the selected descriptions. The separate illustrated archive still opens and exports its existing source bookmarks.

The guide is an original vector illustration of crease regions. Browser captures pair it with the Block 13 CC0 photo by Lezlie Rewatch and manually entered sample descriptions. Tests cover the Chromium camera stream lifecycle with a synthetic device. Actual phone hardware remains untested. ## Recognition evaluation

Run `python -m fate.palm_evaluation labels.json predictions.json` offline. The command reads two version-1 JSON files and emits aggregate metrics without opening, transmitting or exporting photographs. Keep originals and annotations in a restricted local store, with explicit consent and retention terms.

- The labels document contains `label_origin: "independent-human"` and `photos`. Each photo has an opaque `id`, `image_sha256`, pseudonymous `subject`, `license`, independent `reviewer`, condition tags, and a `features` object. Required keys are `palm`, `orientation`, `head`, `heart`, `life`, and `marking`. Labels are present, absent, or JSON null for uncertain; orientation uses left/right/absent/null. Every present spatial label needs normalized XY points in the EXIF-corrected frame. Use a closed outline for palm, a polyline for each crease, and the location of one independently selected mark. Multi-mark detection needs a future schema.
- Predictions use the same photo IDs and digests, a pinned `model_id` and a separate `operator`. Missing suggestions abstain. Coordinates and confidence are optional so incomplete models can be measured, but missing coordinates reduce localized recall. Scores include per-feature precision/recall, negative-control false positives, abstention, maximum bidirectional sampled-point-to-path error, recall within 0.03 normalized frame units, and confidence coverage, Brier error and reliability bins. Scores are broken out by each condition tag. Unknown human labels are excluded from scoring and counted. Synthetic unit cases verify the scorer arithmetic only.
- The Workbench adapter resizes and strips metadata and validates model-returned enums. It requires the visitor to supply handedness, uses a single usable flag, and returns no spatial localization or calibrated probability. Its optional cloud path requires separate consent. Treat its results as candidate labels only. No change was made to Workbench, and the consumer never calls it.
- Test a hand pose detector for open-hand localization and handedness, then train or evaluate a separate crease segmenter on aligned images. Image processing filters can provide an offline baseline for major crease centerlines, while marks require a separate annotation and error analysis. MediaPipe’s anatomical joints help register a crop, but do not label creases or markings. [Google hand landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker)
- Diff-Palm provides a principal-crease representation and synthetic augmentation for palmprint ROIs; phone-photo transfer, rights and marking coverage still need review. THUPALMLAB uses a commercial scanner with research/educational terms and cannot serve as a phone-photo acceptance set. The CC0 intake fixture is licensed for the demonstrated flow and lacks independent feature annotations. [Diff-Palm paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Jin_Diff-Palm_Realistic_Palmprint_Generation_with_Polynomial_Creases_and_Intra-Class_Variation_Controllable_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) · [THUPALMLAB terms](https://ivg.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/dataset/THUPALMLAB.php)
- MPW-180 covers varied lighting and participant-owned smartphones. Its CC BY-NC 4.0 images are limited to academic and non-commercial use, and the paper evaluates biometric matching. It does not establish benchmark labels for the requested crease and marking tasks. [MPW-180 paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/21/11368)

Orientation labels can optionally include `rotation_degrees` for the clockwise angle of the wrist-to-middle-finger axis from vertical up. The scorer reports circular error and the number of reported angles separately from left/right accuracy.

**Release gate is open.** Before suggesting any feature, freeze a subject-disjoint held-out phone corpus and separate calibration split. Include at least 100 distinct held-out participants, 50 negative controls, sufficient positives per feature and phones across lighting, blur, pose, rotation, mirroring, partial crops and skin-tone strata. Obtain blinded human labels from two reviewers and adjudicate disagreements. Freeze thresholds before scoring. For each proposed feature, require precision ≥ 95%, recall ≥ 80% with abstentions counted as misses, calibrated confidence and no condition stratum below 90% precision. For spatial features require ≥ 80% localized recall at the documented tolerance. Keep non-palm false positives ≤ 2%. Report counts and confidence intervals with each estimate. A supported feature may be released separately once it passes; suggestions must still sit outside the confirmed-observation document until a visitor explicitly accepts them. There is no release dataset or passing model result in this block.
